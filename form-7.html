    Form 7 misuse

    Form 7 is the application to object to a name on the electoral roll or have it deleted. These reports cover deletion requests filed in other people's names, bulk objections against particular communities, and the police cases that followed. Each case includes the official response where one was reported.

      How to read this page

      Each link carries a label. Allegation is a claim by a party or politician. Investigation is a newsroom's own reporting or analysis. Ground report means reporters or officials checked on the spot. Police probe covers investigations and arrests. Official response is what the Election Commission or other officials said. Court covers court hearings. Explainer sets out the background.

      An allegation is not a finding. Read the original reports for the evidence and the replies. To add a report, edit js/form7-data.js.
