      How to read this page

      Each link carries a label. Investigation is a newsroom's own reporting. Ground report means reporters spoke to the people affected. Allegation is a claim by a party or politician. Official response is what the Commission or its officers said or did. Court covers court hearings and orders. Parliament covers moves in Parliament.

      An allegation is not a finding. Read the original reports for the evidence and the replies. To add a report, edit js/accountability-data.js.
